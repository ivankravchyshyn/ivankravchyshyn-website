BIOGRAPHY

Ivan Kravchyshyn is a Ukrainian film director and documentary filmmaker with more than three decades of professional experience in film and television.

His work includes four feature films and around 100 documentaries and television productions. His films have been presented at international festivals and industry events including Karlovy Vary, PRIX EUROPA in Berlin, Cottbus, Bergen, Bucharest, Beirut, New York, Odesa and Kinoshock.

His feature film Stop Revolution! competed in the East of the West Competition at the Karlovy Vary International Film Festival, while his documentary In Search of the Ark received the Golden Cedar Grand Prize in Beirut. He was also recognized with Ukraine’s Teletriumph Award for Best Director.

Working across fiction and documentary cinema, he has developed projects from the initial idea and screenplay through directing and cinematography to editing and post-production. His background as a cinematographer and editor has shaped a strongly visual approach to directing, with a particular focus on character, atmosphere and authentic environments.

Since relocating to Germany in 2022, Kravchyshyn has continued his career within the German and European film industry. In 2026, he completed the IHK qualification as Mediengestalter Bild und Ton in Cologne.

Based in North Rhine-Westphalia, he is currently developing new documentary and fiction projects for the European market.
